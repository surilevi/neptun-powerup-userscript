## What This Software Does

This tool automates browser interactions with the Neptun student information system, including:

- **Session keepalive** - refreshes authentication tokens before they expire
- **Automated course enrollment** - clicks enrollment buttons and fills forms
- **Automated exam signup** - tries to enroll saved exam dates
- **Session timeout suppression** - dismisses session timeout dialogs

These behaviors may violate your university's acceptable-use policy. The first-run consent dialog lists these behaviors before the features activate.
