## What This Software Does

This tool automates interactions with the Neptun student information system, including:

- **Session keepalive** - refreshes authentication tokens before expiry to reduce unexpected session timeouts
- **Automated course enrollment** - programmatically clicks enrollment buttons and fills forms
- **Automated exam signup** - enrolls for saved exam dates on the user's behalf
- **Session timeout suppression** - automatically dismisses session expiration dialogs

These behaviors may violate your university's acceptable use policy. A first-run consent dialog informs users of these behaviors before any features activate.
